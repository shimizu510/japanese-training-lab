# Japanese Training Lab promotion launch plan

## The promise to repeat everywhere

**Learn Japanese for free—from your first hiragana to JLPT N1. No signup required. Practice on the web, talk with Yuji on TikTok, and continue through adventure in DraMap.**

Do not position the placement test as the main TikTok activity. Yuji's TikTok is a conversation space. The natural loop is:

1. Learn one useful word or phrase on Japanese Training Lab.
2. Join Yuji's TikTok LIVE and try using it in the conversation.
3. Return to the site's daily plan or continue in DraMap.

## Launch checklist

### 1. Search discovery

- Add `https://shimizu510.github.io/japanese-training-lab/` as a URL-prefix property in Google Search Console.
- Submit `https://shimizu510.github.io/japanese-training-lab/sitemap.xml`.
- Inspect the homepage plus one representative course page from each level and request indexing if needed.
- Add the site to Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the same sitemap.
- Check indexed-page and search-query reports monthly; do not treat sitemap submission as a ranking guarantee.

### 2. TikTok profile and LIVE

Suggested bio:

> 🇯🇵 Talk with Yuji in Japan  
> 📚 Learn Japanese free, then try it LIVE ↓

Natural LIVE lines:

> What Japanese word did you learn today? Try it with me.

> If you need a word for our next conversation, my free Japanese Training Lab is in the bio. No signup.

> Learn it on the site, then come back and say it to Yuji.

Use these only when they fit the ongoing conversation. Do not turn the LIVE into a placement-test stream or promise an endless quiz-video schedule.

## How the three websites work together

- **Japanese Training Lab:** free structured learning and practice.
- **Japan Unfolded:** evergreen cultural, travel and manga context that gives learners a reason to understand Japan.
- **Japan Daily Brief:** current context and recurring Japanese words that give learners a reason to return.

Link between them only where the reader's next step is genuinely relevant. Future language-related articles should point to a matching practice course when one exists; the learning site should point readers to the two publications for authentic motivation and context.

## Measures for the first 30 days

Record a baseline before promotion, then review weekly:

- Google Search Console: indexed pages, impressions, clicks, queries and course-page entrances.
- Website: visits to Courses, starts of a lesson, clicks to TikTok, clicks to DraMap, and return visits.
- TikTok: bio-link clicks and comments mentioning the site or a learned phrase.
- Outreach: accepted resource listings and useful feedback, not just raw post views.

Avoid adding heavy analytics or marketing scripts until a privacy-respecting measurement choice is made. Search Console plus outbound-link and lesson-start events are enough for the first iteration.
