The training cycle

Lessons separate understanding from automatic processing. Knowing the answer is the start; retrieving it quickly and using it in context is the target.

01

Understand

Meet the sound, form, meaning or grammar in a clear context.

02

Retrieve

Recall the same knowledge from more than one direction.

03

Process

Recognize it quickly inside speech and sentences.

04

Use

Apply it to a response, reading or practical task.

05

Review

Return when memory needs effort, before it disappears.
